Community rules

Chirping is part of hockey. Abuse is not. In any Rostric chat, profile or team name you may not post:

  • harassment, threats or bullying;
  • hate speech or slurs aimed at anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability;
  • sexual content, or anything involving minors;
  • spam, scams or other people's private information;
  • anything illegal.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Content that breaks these rules can be removed, and people who post it can be removed from teams and leagues.

Reporting and blocking

Press and hold any message to report it or to block its author. Reports go to the room's captains or commissioners, who can remove the message and the person, and to us; we can remove a message for everyone when they do not. Blocking hides everything that person posts from you immediately and stops their notifications. You can also report content to us through Help and contact; we act on reports within 24 hours.

What you write and enter is yours. By posting it you allow Rostric to store it and show it to the members of your teams and leagues, which is the whole point of the app. You can delete your messages, and your account, at any time.
