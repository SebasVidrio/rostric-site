Privacy policy

The short version: Rostric has no ads, no tracking and no analytics that follow you. What you put in is shown to your team, stored in Apple's iCloud, and you can delete it.

Who we are

Where it is stored

Rostric has no servers of its own. Team data is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in a database that belongs to the Rostric app, and is tied to your iCloud account by an identifier Apple creates for this app only. We never see your Apple Account email, name or password. A short private record in your own iCloud keeps the list of teams you are on, so that a new phone brings them back; nobody else can read it.

A team's data can be read by people who have that team's invite code. Treat the code like a key: share it with your team, not the internet. Only you can change or delete what you wrote.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether Rostric Pro has been bought; it receives a random app identifier and your purchase history for Rostric, not your name or payment details. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Rostric does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data. Photos are only read when you pick one yourself.
  • We do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Notifications

Game reminders are scheduled on your own iPhone. Notifications about new chat messages and schedule changes are delivered by Apple's push service from iCloud. You can switch them off in Rostric's settings or in the iPhone's Settings app.

Deleting your data

In Rostric: Team → Settings → Delete my account and data. This removes your chat messages, clears your profile details, takes you off every team and deletes your private iCloud record. Goals, assists and penalties you were credited with stay in your team's records under "Former player", because they are part of the team's history. If you cannot use the app, contact us and we will do it for you.

Children

Rostric is for adult recreational leagues and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the bottom of this page shows the latest version.
