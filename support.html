Common questions

How do I get my team on Rostric?
One person starts the team and becomes captain. Open the Team tab, tap "Invite the bench" and share the six-character code. Everyone else chooses "Join a team" and types it in. Joining is free.

Do players have to pay?
No. Joining any number of teams, replying, the schedule, chat, final scores, standings and every stat are free. Rostric Pro (one purchase, no subscription) is for the people who run the team: live goal-by-goal scoring and box scores, setting lines, brackets, dues and a personal game log. One Pro on the bench is enough to live-score games for everyone.

How do I run a league?
Choose "Run a league" when you first open Rostric, or, if you captain a team, Team → Join a league → "Or run it yourself". Send each captain the six-character league code; they enter it under Team → Join a league. Then make the whole season's schedule from the league page. Leagues are free for the 2026–27 season, and leagues started this season stay free through 2027–28.

Which sports does Rostric support?
Hockey, soccer, basketball, softball, volleyball, flag football and kickball. You pick the sport when you start a team, and each team keeps its own: its positions, scoring, stats and colour follow it. Playing on a hockey team and a softball team is fine.

How do I ping the team, or one person?
Captains, alternates and coaches tap the megaphone on Home and send to everyone or to the teammates they choose. To nudge people who haven't answered about a game, use "Ping them" under To do. Teammates get a notification if they've allowed Rostric's notifications.

Can I load the whole schedule at once?
Yes. Schedule → + → Import, then choose a calendar file, a calendar link, a screenshot of the league's schedule, or pasted text. Check the games Rostric found, untick any you don't want, and add them. For a game at the same time every week, add one game and turn on "Repeat every week".

How do I get the schedule into my own calendar?
The share button at the top of Schedule ("Add to my calendar") sends the season to your calendar; a single game's page has "Add to Calendar".

How do I add a photo or a team logo?
Your photo: open your player card → Edit profile → Choose a photo. A team logo: Team → Edit team. Teammates see them through iCloud. Anyone can report a photo or logo from its … menu, and captains can remove a teammate's photo.

It says "Sign in to iCloud to sync".
Rostric keeps your team in sync through iCloud. Open the iPhone's Settings app, sign in to your Apple Account, and make sure iCloud is on.

I got a new phone. Where's my team?
Sign in to the same Apple Account and open Rostric. Your teams come back by themselves.

I bought Pro and it's locked.
Team → Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I report a message or block someone?
Press and hold the message. Reports go to the room's captains or commissioners and to us; either can remove the message. Blocking hides that person's messages and their notifications.

How do I delete my account?
Team → Settings → Delete my account and data.
