Your beer league, sorted.

Rostric runs an adult rec league team so the group text doesn't have to: who's in, where the game is, one chat for everyone, and stats like a real sports app. Hockey, soccer, basketball, softball, volleyball, flag football and kickball, each with its own positions, stats and standings.

Who's in. One tap to reply. Everyone sees whether there are enough players, and a goalie or keeper where the sport needs one.

Pings. Captains ping the whole team or just the players they pick, and chase replies with one button.

One chat. The team, the captains, and league rooms for captains and spares.

The schedule. Import the league's calendar, a screenshot or pasted text. Send it, or one game, to your own calendar.

Real stats. Box scores, standings, leaders, playoff brackets and your own player card. Final scores are always free.

Run the league. A league code for every captain, the whole season's schedule in one go, standings and playoffs. Free for the 2026–27 season.

Pay once, not monthly. Players never pay. Rostric Pro is a single purchase for the people who run the team. No ads.
